# Skill: Formula11 Design System

## Purpose

Apply the official Formula11 (F11) visual identity when generating user interfaces, dashboards, tournament screens, scoreboards, rankings, brackets, registration forms, admin panels, analytics, and reports.

Formula11 is a modern pickleball tournament scoring and management platform inspired by the professionalism and performance-oriented nature of Formula One.

The design language should feel:

- Professional
- Modern
- Athletic
- Premium
- Clean
- Data-focused
- Easy on the eyes
- Tournament-grade

Never use overly playful, cartoonish, gaming, neon cyberpunk, or flashy esports styles.

---

# Brand Identity

## Brand Name

Formula11

## Short Name

F11

## Tagline

Score Fast. Rank Fair. Play to 11.

---

# Design Principles

1. Information first.
2. Tournament data must be easy to scan.
3. Rankings and scores must be immediately visible.
4. Visual hierarchy must be clear.
5. White space is preferred over excessive decoration.
6. Components should look modern and enterprise-grade.
7. Use subtle color accents rather than overwhelming saturation.
8. Focus on readability for tournament officials and players.

# Tournament Components

## Match Card

Must display:

- Team A
- Team B
- Court
- Schedule
- Current Score
- Match Status

Status colors:

Pending:
Gray

Live:
Brand Green

Completed:
Success Green

---

## Rankings Table

Columns:

- Rank
- Team
- Wins
- Losses
- Points For
- Points Against
- Differential

Top 3 rows should receive subtle emphasis.

---

## Brackets

Style:

- Clean lines
- Modern connectors
- Spacious layout

Do not use skeuomorphic tournament graphics.

---

## Dashboard Widgets

Recommended:

- Active Tournaments
- Ongoing Matches
- Completed Matches
- Registered Players
- Available Courts

---

# Logo Usage

Logo style must be:

- Minimalist
- Geometric
- Flat
- Modern

Avoid:

- Pickleball paddles
- Pickleball balls
- Mascots
- Complex illustrations
- 3D effects

The preferred icon style is the circular F11 monogram.

---

# Animation Rules

Use:

- 150ms–250ms transitions
- Subtle hover effects
- Soft fade animations

Avoid:

- Bounce effects
- Flashing animations
- Excessive motion

---

# Overall Feel

When generating Formula11 interfaces, imagine a blend of:

- Formula One timing systems
- Notion cleanliness
- Linear simplicity
- Stripe professionalism
- Modern sports analytics dashboards

Everything should feel premium, efficient, and tournament-ready.
